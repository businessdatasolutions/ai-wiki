On-screen content (machine-read):

```text
HubSpot · AEO
Six signals answer engines use to decide which content to cite:

1 Clear, direct answers | Lead with the answer. Answer engines reward content that gets to the point.
2 Original expertise & unique POV | Unique data can't be replicated - so it gets cited. Proprietary stats, first-hand case studies, and original analysis are prioritized by AI systems.
3 Entity richness | Named things tell AI what you know. Products, industries, use cases, and integrations signal domain depth.
4 Full-funnel coverage | Gaps in your funnel mean lost citations. AI answers prompts at every stage. If you only cover one phase, competitors fill the rest.
5 Structured, crawlable pages | If AI can't parse it, it won't cite it. FAQ headers, clean HTML, and schema markup make content easy for AI to extract.
6 Freshness & recency | Freshness signals relevance. Answer engines favor content that reflects the current state of a topic.
```
