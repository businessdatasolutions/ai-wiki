## [7:18] Evolution of TPU architecture

[7:18] So what truly defines the anatomy of a TPU? It comes down to six fundamental principles. Instead of hardware managing thousands of tiny threads, we use fewer larger cores so our software compiler can plan out execution without thread schedulers or dynamic branching logic.
[7:37] Then we use systolic arrays, which work by streaming data continuously through a physical grid of multipliers and reusing it cycle after cycle instead of fetching it from registers for every operation.
[7:51] Rather than relying on hardware caches, we use compiler-controlled memory to schedule exactly when data moves for fewer latency spikes.
[8:00] Optical circuit switches give us a way to connect thousands of chips using beams of light reflected by tiny mirrors, avoiding the power draw and drop packets of electrical switches.
[8:11] We prioritize range over precision using compact number formats like bfloat16 and native FP4 to execute more math per watt of energy while maintaining strict bit wise determinism.
[8:26] And our liquid cooling cold plates sit alongside the silicon to prevent chips from overheating so we can pack more compute into each rack and run at peak speed continuously.
[8:38] These principles are how TPUs deliver incredible price performance. Let's step into the lab to see the real thing.

## [10:47] Agentic inference innovations

[10:47] But most organizations aren't building new models and that brings us to the thinking part of an agentic stack, inference.
