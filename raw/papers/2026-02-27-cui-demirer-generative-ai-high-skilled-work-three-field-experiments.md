## Summary of the publisher abstract

Three randomized controlled trials at **Microsoft, Accenture, and an anonymous Fortune 100 electronics manufacturer** measuring the effect of GitHub Copilot access on developer output.

## Key numbers

- **4,867 developers** across three field experiments
- Headline effect: **a 26.08% increase (standard error 10.3%) in completed tasks** for developers granted access
- Heterogeneity: **less experienced / more junior developers adopted the tool at higher rates and gained more**
- Pre-registered as AEARCTR-0014530
