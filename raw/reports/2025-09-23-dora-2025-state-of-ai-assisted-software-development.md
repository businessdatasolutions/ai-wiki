## Sample

Nearly **5,000 technology professionals** surveyed worldwide, plus **over 100 hours** of qualitative data.

## Adoption

- **90% of respondents use AI at work** (up from 76% in the 2024 report)
- **>80% believe AI has increased their productivity**
- **70% report trusting AI-generated code** — meaning **30% report little or none**
- **90% of organizations have adopted at least one platform**

## Central thesis

> "AI doesn't fix a team; it amplifies what's already there."

Strong teams get more efficient. Struggling teams find AI surfaces the problems they already had.

## The throughput / stability split — the load-bearing finding

- **Changed from 2024:** the 2025 data shows a **positive** relationship between AI adoption and both **software delivery throughput** and **product performance**. (The 2024 baseline had associated each 25% rise in AI adoption with roughly a **1.5% throughput drop** and a **7.2% stability drop**.)
- **Unchanged from 2024:** "**AI adoption does continue to have a negative relationship with software delivery stability.**"
- The mechanism, in the report's words: "AI accelerates software development, but that acceleration can expose weaknesses downstream. Without robust control systems, like strong automated testing, mature version control practices, and fast feedback loops, an increase in change volume leads to instability."

So throughput turned positive year over year while **stability did not**. Volume is no longer the problem; the absorptive capacity of the delivery system is.

## Seven team profiles

Cluster analysis yields seven archetypes, including **"Foundational challenges"** (survival mode: low performance, high instability, high burnout and friction) and **"Harmonious high achievers"** (strong across well-being, product outcomes, and delivery).

## DORA AI Capabilities Model

Seven capabilities that amplify AI's positive impact: clarify and socialise AI policies; connect AI to internal context; prioritise foundational practices; fortify your safety nets; invest in your internal platform; focus on end-users; (plus one further capability in the model visual).

## Three framing insights

1. **User-centricity is a prerequisite** — AI is most useful when pointed at a clear problem
2. **Platform engineering is the foundation** — internal platform quality correlates directly with the ability to unlock AI value
3. Teams are measurably improving at integrating AI into their workflows
