## What this is

The newsletter write-up of the *How I AI* episode "How I manage 15 AI agents 24/7 as a solo founder." Same date, same content, different surface: the video carries the full conversation, this post carries the framing and the takeaway list.

## Guest

Ryan Carson — five-time founder, currently **solo** founder of **Untangle**, a B2B SaaS platform for family-law firms. Previously co-founded **Treehouse** (online coding education). Publishes his monthly AI tool spend openly on X.

## Stated takeaways

1. **15 concurrent Devin agents managed with a folder system and a handwritten paper list** — not a dashboard. Buckets span engineering, customer success, and investor updates.
2. **The Watchdog playbook** — built to replace a dedicated customer-success function by monitoring every law-firm account.
3. **The "Land PR" skill** — closes the loop on ~40 daily pull requests with no QA team reviewing any of them manually.
4. **Tool split:** Devin as the primary cloud agent, Codex for pair-programming and long grinding front-end work, Claude Design → Markdown → Codex for design systems.
5. **"More AI output is actually the wrong goal"** — the episode's contrarian centre: quantity of shipped code is not the constraint on product quality.
6. **Hiring without phone screens or interviews** — candidates submit a full-screen video of themselves building a feature; the hire signal is *how good an agent manager they are*.
7. **Meeting real customers in person drove the product pivot** that found PMF.

## Cost figures quoted in the title and body

$5,000/month on Devin initially, rising to **$20,000 in one month**; Carson subsequently negotiated credits from Cognition.
