## What the company does

AI-powered legal workflow software for **divorce attorneys and family-law firms**. Positioned as "AI for divorce attorneys," addressing three named pain points: **discovery chaos, paralegal shortages, and administrative busy work**. Serves cases from standard matters to high-asset and complex divorces.

## Founder

**Ryan Carson**, CEO and Founder — 25 years building technology products and communities, including **Treehouse**, **DropSend** and **Carsonified**.

## Origin

Carson launched Untangle after watching his parents and two sisters go through "painful and confusing divorces," seeking to "make the divorce process more humane, less costly, and far less overwhelming for families."
