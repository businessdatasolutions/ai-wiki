# PwC 29th Global CEO Survey, 2026 (press release)

## TL;DR

[[PwC]]'s annual survey of 4,454 CEOs in 95 countries, fielded 30 September to 10 November 2025 and released at Davos on 19 January 2026. This page rests on PwC's wire release; the report itself was not reachable.

1. **One in eight report both kinds of benefit.** *"Only one-in-eight (12%) CEOs say AI has delivered both cost and revenue benefits."* 33% report gains in *either* cost or revenue; **56%** *"have seen no significant financial benefit to date."* The three sum to 101%, so the 33% reads as one benefit, not both; at least one benefit is then about 45%.
2. **Depth goes with results.** CEOs reporting both gains are *"two to three times more likely"* to have embedded AI extensively across products and services, demand generation and strategic decision-making.
3. **Confidence is at a five-year low.** 30% are confident about revenue growth over the next 12 months (2025: 38%; 2022: 56%).
4. **Few can stop what does not work.** *"Only one in four CEOs"* report disciplined processes to stop underperforming initiatives, tolerance of high innovation risk, or an innovation centre.

## Method and definitions

- **Weighting:** *"The global and regional figures in this report are weighted proportionally to country nominal GDP."* Industry and country figures are unweighted. The 12% and 56% are global figures, so large economies count for more than their share of respondents.
- **What counts as benefit:** the CEO's own judgement of AI's cost and revenue effects. The release gives no question wording, period or threshold (such as a share of EBIT).
- **"Strong AI foundations"** is illustrated, not defined (*"such as Responsible AI frameworks..."*). The *"nearly four percentage points higher profit margins"* comes from *"separate PwC analysis,"* not this survey.

## Scope and reliability

Self-report by CEOs; the question wording is not published. PwC sells AI and transformation services. The sample is large and the GDP weighting is stated. Figures in secondary coverage (30% higher revenue from AI, 26% lower costs) are not in the release and were not checked.
