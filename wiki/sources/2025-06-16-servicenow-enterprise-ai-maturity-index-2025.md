# ServiceNow Enterprise AI Maturity Index 2025: Europe, Middle East and Netherlands findings

## TL;DR

ServiceNow's second Enterprise AI Maturity Index, built with Oxford Economics. This page rests on ServiceNow's Dutch press release (16 June 2025) and the EMEA data sheet attached to it.

1. **The average score fell.** *"The global Enterprise AI Maturity Index score is 35, down from 44 last year"* (data sheet). The score runs from 0 to 100. Every country in the data sheet fell.
2. **The Netherlands fell furthest in the region.** From 46 to 34, a drop of 12 points (data sheet). The Dutch release gives the earlier score as 45: *"vorig jaar scoorde ons land nog 45"* ("last year our country still scored 45").
3. **Very few reach the top stage.** *"Slechts 6% weet met zijn AI-projecten tot de hoogste fase te komen: de augmentatie-fase. In Nederland ligt dat percentage nog lager, slechts 2%"* (Only 6% reach the highest stage, augmentation. In the Netherlands only 2%).
4. **Many use cases, stalled governance.** 47% of organisations in Europe and the Middle East launched more than 100 AI use cases last year (Netherlands 50%). Significant progress on AI data governance fell from 45% to 42% (Netherlands 40%). 15% already use agentic AI.

## Method and definitions

- **Sample:** 4,473 *"senior leaders and IT decision makers"* in 16 countries and 11 industries (data sheet); the release says *"bijna 4.500"* (almost 4,500). 1,950 are in nine countries in Europe and the Middle East. **Netherlands n=257.**
- **Partner:** Oxford Economics.
- **Scoring:** a *"proprietary indexing model."* Five components: AI strategy and leadership, workflow integration, talent and workforce, AI governance, and AI investment. *"Op basis van de antwoorden heeft ServiceNow elke organisatie een AI-volwassenheidsscore gegeven tussen de 0 en 100"* (From the answers, ServiceNow gave each organisation a maturity score from 0 to 100). Weights and items are not published in these documents.
- **Stages:** "augmentation" is the highest; the others are not listed. **Fieldwork dates** are not given.

## Scope and reliability

A vendor index: ServiceNow sells an enterprise AI platform. The model is proprietary, so the fall from 44 to 35 cannot be traced to its causes: real change, a new sample, or a bar that rises with the technology (the data sheet: *"maturity is becoming a moving target"*). The sample size (4,473) is now confirmed. The release and the data sheet disagree on the Netherlands' 2024 score (45 or 46).
