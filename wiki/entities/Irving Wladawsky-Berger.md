# Irving Wladawsky-Berger

Co-host, with **[[Allan Tate]]**, of the [[MIT Sloan CIO Symposium]]'s inquiry framework and weekly podcast series. ASR badly garbles his name across the wiki's two symposium sources ("Irving Vladaskki Burgerer" on the keynote transcript; "Irving Velasi Burger" on the podcast transcript) — identification is confident on content grounds (same co-host role, same symposium, same voice across both transcripts) despite the ASR noise. Writes a weekly blog since 2005 (formerly syndicated in the *Wall Street Journal* CIO Journal); has sat on the Linux Foundation's research advisory board for roughly five years and has been associated with the Foundation since its 2000 founding. In the Collective Sensemaking series he says he holds a PhD in physics, and that at IBM he worked on bringing the internet to market in the mid-1990s; he uses ChatGPT as the editor of his weekly blog.
