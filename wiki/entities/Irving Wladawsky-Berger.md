# Irving Wladawsky-Berger

Co-host, with **[[Allan Tate]]**, of the [[MIT Sloan CIO Symposium]]'s inquiry framework and weekly podcast series. ASR badly garbles his name across the wiki's two symposium sources ("Irving Vladaskki Burgerer" on the keynote transcript; "Irving Velasi Burger" on the podcast transcript) — identification is confident on content grounds (same co-host role, same symposium, same voice across both transcripts) despite the ASR noise. Writes a weekly blog since 2005 (formerly syndicated in the *Wall Street Journal* CIO Journal); has sat on the Linux Foundation's research advisory board for roughly five years and has been associated with the Foundation since its 2000 founding.
