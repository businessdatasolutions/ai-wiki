# Steven Brovich

**AWS Enterprise Strategist**, an Amazon employee since August 1999. Appears in the wiki under two ASR-transcribed spellings of the same surname — **"Steven Brovich"** ([[2026-06-12-aws-leaders-guide-advanced-team-structures-agentic-world|AWS Summit Sydney, 12 Jun 2026]]) and **"Stephen Brozovich"** ([[2025-12-02-allen-brozovich-davis-aws-reinvent-advanced-team-structures|AWS re:Invent 2025, 2 Dec 2025]]) — treated here as the same person on the strength of matching biographical detail (Amazon since 1999, AWS Enterprise Strategist role, same recurring "advanced team structures" talk franchise) rather than a confirmed cross-source identity check; both spellings are recorded as aliases per [§Author-entity promotion](../../CLAUDE.md#author-entity-promotion).

At re:Invent 2025, Brozovich presents Amazon's internal organizational-science research — a "team of organizational scientists" advising across 1,500+ organizations annually — including the Day-After-Tomorrow work-time framework (crediting Peter Hinssen), four mental-model shifts for the agentic era (crediting Ishit Vachhrajani), a five-phase business-lifecycle organizational-alignment model, and an asymmetric human/AI resource-allocation framework across four work types. At AWS Summit Sydney, as "Steven Brovich," he independently delivers the *later, revised* edition of Jonathan Allen's "advanced team structures" keynote — same USE/COMPOSE/BUILD, hourglass-organization, expert-generalist, and Singapore-governance content Allen delivers in the London edition, plus quantified pricing-scissors economics and the Model A/B/C organizational taxonomy.
