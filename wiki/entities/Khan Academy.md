# Khan Academy

Free education nonprofit platform, ~200 million learners/year, founded and led by CEO **Sal Khan**. Built one of the first widely-deployed AI tutors (Khanmigo). First entered the wiki via [[2026-07-14-khan-academy-ceo-the-real-ai-opportunity-is-in-boring-industries-sal-khan|Sal Khan's Silicon Valley Girl interview]] (14 July 2026) — treat firm-specific figures ($1.2M/year Anthropic spend, 350-person team, velocity estimates) as first-person CEO testimony from a single interview, not independently audited data.

## Appears in this wiki via

- [[2026-07-14-khan-academy-ceo-the-real-ai-opportunity-is-in-boring-industries-sal-khan]] — Sal Khan describes Khan Academy's internal AI transformation: a **350-person team** (two-thirds product), a **$1.2M/year run rate** with [[Anthropic]] (reportedly "the top of the stack" per an internal Anthropic usage report on agentic code review), AI connectors reaching Slack/Gmail/docs with human-in-the-loop guardrails, designers and PMs given **full dev environments** to blend into engineering work, a hackathon-vibe-coded feature shipped in about a month instead of a school year, and a subjective **50-100% velocity increase** versus 2-3 years ago. Explicit no-AI-layoffs policy: *"If we could do three times more with the same resources, we will do three times more... that would never be the catalyst for layoffs."* Also the launch of the **Content Institute** — a joint venture with **TED** (Khan succeeds Chris Anderson as TED's "vision steward") and **ETS**: an accreditation-pending, competency-based bachelor's/master's degree capped at $10,000, built around ETS's five-skill durable-skills framework (communication, collaboration, creativity, critical thinking, + a leadership sub-framework), assessed via group simulations and portfolios with six launch corporate co-design partners (McKinsey, Bain, Google, Microsoft, Replit, Accenture).
