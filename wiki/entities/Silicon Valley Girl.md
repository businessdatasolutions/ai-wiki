# Silicon Valley Girl

YouTube channel and interview podcast hosted by **Marina Mogilko**, aimed at a general audience of founders and professionals. Episodes are long-form interviews with technology leaders, often sponsored (the Chesky episode is sponsored by [[HubSpot]]), and edited with explanatory cards and product inserts. The channel promotes a companion newsletter, *Future Proof*.
