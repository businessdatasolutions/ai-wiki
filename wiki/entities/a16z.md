## The operator episode (added 2026-09-16)

- [[2026-08-10-maza-a16z-kavak-rebuilding-a-company-around-ai]] — hosts **Angela Strange** and **Gabriel Vasquez** with **Alejandro Maza Ayala**, Chief Product & AI Officer at [[Kavak]] (10 Aug 2026). Unlike most a16z material in the wiki, which is partner-authored framework or founder advice, this is an **operator account of a completed transformation with figures attached**: 96% of customer interactions and 95% of transactions handled by agents, 100–200k agents instantiated daily, and a deliberate demolition of two years of working multi-agent architecture after the Opus 4.5 release.

The usual a16z caveat applies with force: this is a venture firm's podcast, the format is promotional, and every figure is self-reported and unaudited. The **architecture reasoning** is the durable contribution — see [[agent-harness]], [[multi-agent-failure-modes]] and [[micro-productivity-trap]].
