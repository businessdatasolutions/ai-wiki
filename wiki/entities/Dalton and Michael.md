# Dalton + Michael

A conversation show on YouTube hosted by [[Dalton Caldwell]] and **Michael Seibel**, both longtime [[Y Combinator]] partners. Seibel co-founded Justin.tv and Twitch; Caldwell founded imeem and App.net. Episodes are unscripted two-person discussions of startup advice drawn from YC office hours, usually framed around founder mistakes. The show is sponsored by **Standard Capital**, the hosts' own firm.

The channel's `author:` value is written `Dalton + Michael` on source pages, following the video convention that the channel is the author. This page uses the spelled-out filename to keep the slug safe. Michael Seibel has no entity page yet because he appears only as a presenter, never as a frontmatter author.
