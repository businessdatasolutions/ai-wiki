# Allan Tate

**Executive Chair of the [[MIT Sloan CIO Symposium]]**. ASR renders his first name "Alan" in every symposium transcript; the channel's own episode descriptions spell it "Allan". Co-creator, with **[[Irving Wladawsky-Berger]]**, of the symposium's **inquiry framework** and its weekly podcast series — both born from the two men's ongoing conversations about how CIOs should think, not just what they should do.

## Wiki contributions

- [[2026-05-28-from-event-to-ecosystem-rethinking-how-technology-leaders-build-knowledge-in-the-ai-era]] — delivers the pre-event keynote opening the 23rd annual (2026) symposium: the inquiry framework's origin story, the "orchestration not adoption" reframe, and the CIO-as-designer-of-intelligence-systems role redefinition.
- [[2026-07-10-when-ai-becomes-common-organizational-capability-becomes-the-advantage-ep-16]] — co-hosts Episode 16 of the symposium's weekly podcast with Wladawsky-Berger; opens with symposium housekeeping (2027 event dates, the summer inquiry series) before steering the conversation through the Linux Foundation tech-talent report, Davenport's organizational-capability thesis, and CIO priorities.
- [[2026-08-26-the-mystery-of-ai-connecting-the-dots-ep-19]] — co-hosts part 1 of the four-part Collective Sensemaking series; describes his symposium-planning assistant, whose thread state and memories are saved and handed to the next thread ("thread number five… booted up from… thread number four").
- [[2026-09-02-from-conversation-to-institutional-understanding-ep-20]] — describes the workflow he built for the 2026 symposium report: task specifications, one memory record per session, an index, a synthesis across records, human review. Names the result institutional understanding and predicts it lowers the cost of institutional self-correction.
- [[2026-09-15-from-debate-to-inquiry-designing-conversations-worth-preserving-ep-21]] — tells the long version of the Inquiry Framework, which he says he primarily developed: why debate did not suit executives, the question rewrites, three lenses, the tiered "layer cake".
- [[2026-10-07-from-conversations-to-an-executive-learning-architecture-ep-22]] — names the whole system the Executive Learning Architecture and states its limits: a small-team prototype that shows neither enterprise-scale learning nor better decisions. Mentions a background in IT architecture (systems for Boeing and Walmart).
