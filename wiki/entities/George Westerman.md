# George Westerman

**MIT Sloan School of Management** researcher and teacher on digital and AI transformation. He calls himself *"a reformed engineer"* who now teaches management. He co-wrote *Leading Digital* (Harvard Business Review Press). His frameworks appear in [[MIT Sloan Management Review]]:
- *"The First Law of Digital Innovation"* (2019): *"Technology changes quickly. Organizations change much more slowly"*;
- *"Building Digital-Ready Culture in Traditional Organizations"* (2019, with Deborah Soule and Anand Eswaran);
- *"The New Elements of Digital Transformation"* (with Didier Bonnet);
- *"Generate Value from GenAI with Small 't' Transformations"* (January 2025, with Melissa Webster).

He also co-wrote HBR articles with Maryam Alavi (2023) and with Sam Ransbotham and Chiara Farronato. These titles are from his own reading-list slide; none of the articles is ingested.

Promoted to an entity page on 26 November 2026, at his second appearance. He had been listed as Dangling on the first. He is a speaker, not an `author:`, on both pages: the [§Author-entity promotion](../../CLAUDE.md#author-entity-promotion) rule does not require the page. It was made because both sources give his own views at length.

## What he contributes on the pages

- **[[2026-06-11-mit-smr-agentic-ai-what-leaders-wish-they-knew-sooner|MIT SMR / CIO Symposium panel (Jun 2026)]]:** agents *"aren't ready for prime time"*. The label is being applied to unsophisticated automation, *"increasing the hype without necessarily increasing the value."* His forward move: *"automate first, then put humans in the right places,"* and rebuild processes around desired outcomes rather than adding tools to the steps of the existing workflow.
- **[[2026-08-26-westerman-mit-enterprise-ai-forum-making-ai-real|Making AI Real, MIT Enterprise AI Forum (talk 15 Apr 2026, published Aug 2026)]]:** the main source. It contains:
  - **six levers**, each with a question and a −5 to +5 self-score: shared ambition; governance and value capture; technology platform, culture and talent;
  - **directed emergence** in place of planning and execution;
  - the **AI growth slope** in three levels (individual productivity; specialized roles and tasks; products and processes);
  - **AI-ready values** (speed, impact, autonomy, openness, plus ethics and empathy);
  - cases: Rio Tinto, Dentsu Creative, Newport News Shipbuilding and HCA Healthcare.

## Recurring positions

- **The bottleneck is organisational.** *"Technology is NOT the problem. Transformation is."* (2026); rebuild for outcomes instead of adding tools to existing steps (both sources).
- **Governance as steering.** *"Is your governance more the steering? Or is it more the brakes?"* (2026); human oversight placed where it matters (both sources).
