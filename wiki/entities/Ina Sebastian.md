# Ina Sebastian

**Ina M. Sebastian** is a research scientist at [[MIT CISR]], the MIT Sloan research center. Her research covers digital partnering, value creation and value capture. She co-authors CISR's research briefings with [[Peter Weill]] and [[Stephanie Woerner]], and reads the briefings for CISR's audio edition.

## Sources in this wiki

- [[2025-10-16-weill-cisr-business-models-ai-era|Weill, Sebastian, Woerner & Benedict, *Business Models in the AI Era* (MIT CISR Research Briefing, Oct 2025)]]: co-author of the AI-era business-model framework (Existing+, Customer Proxy, Modular Curator, Orchestrator) and narrator of its audio edition.
- [[2026-04-28-mit-sloan-ai-maturity|MIT Sloan, *How to boost your organization's AI maturity level* (Oct 2025)]]: listed among the researchers (`research_by:`) behind the Enterprise AI Maturity model and the Four S framework, summarised by Kristin Burnham.

Promoted on 2026-11-07, when the CISR business-models briefing became her second source. The first lists her as a researcher rather than an author, so the [author-entity promotion rule](../../CLAUDE.md#author-entity-promotion) was applied to research credit, not only to `author:` fields.
