# Hila Lifshitz

Management and innovation researcher at Warwick Business School and the HBS AI Institute. Promoted on the **second-source rule**: she is a co-author on **both** of the corpus's [[Fabrizio Dell'Acqua|Dell'Acqua]] field experiments, which together form the wiki's strongest empirical line on generative AI and knowledge work.

Her research background is in open innovation and how technology reshapes knowledge boundaries in professional work, which is visible in what the two papers chose to measure: not just output quality but **who is able to produce what kind of work**, and what happens to professional expertise when the boundary moves.

## Appears in this wiki via

- [[2026-04-28-dellacqua-jagged-technological-frontier]] — the BCG field experiment that named the [[jagged-frontier]], measuring individual consultants against an uneven capability boundary.
- [[2026-06-12-dellacqua-cybernetic-teammate-field-experiment-genai-teamwork]] — the preregistered 791-person Procter & Gamble experiment on whether AI can substitute for a human teammate; its **silo-dissolution** result (professionals producing balanced work outside their own function) is the expertise-boundary question her prior work sets up.

Co-authors in this corpus: [[Fabrizio Dell'Acqua]], [[Ethan Mollick]], [[Karim Lakhani]].
