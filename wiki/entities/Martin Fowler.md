# Martin Fowler

**Martin Fowler** is a British software developer, author, and speaker, **Chief Scientist at [[Thoughtworks]]**, and one of the most-cited voices in software design — known for *Refactoring*, *Patterns of Enterprise Application Architecture*, and the *Agile Manifesto*. His site **martinfowler.com** is a long-running reference for patterns and practices in the field. He describes his own career as a string of dismissive labels — "object guy," "patterns guy," "agile guy," "architecture guy" — which is itself part of the argument of the source that promotes him here.
