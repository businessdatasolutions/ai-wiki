# TechCrunch

A **technology news publication** covering startups and venture capital. It runs the *Equity* podcast and the annual *Disrupt* conference, whose stage sessions it publishes on its YouTube channel. Its articles are cited in other wiki sources (for example [[2026-10-17-roth-verge-websites-closing-doors-to-ai-agents|The Verge's report]] on sites blocking AI agents cites a TechCrunch interview with Yelp).
