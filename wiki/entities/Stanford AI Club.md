# Stanford AI Club

A **student-run AI organisation at Stanford University** that hosts a speaker series and publishes the talks on its YouTube channel. The format so far: a fireside conversation led by two student moderators, followed by audience Q&A, aimed at students who want to build companies or careers in AI. The 2026–27 series opened with [[Sequoia Capital]]'s Alfred Lin, moderated by Jason Zhang and Tanvir Bhathal of the club's leadership.

## Wiki contributions

- [[2026-05-05-stanford-ai-club-chamath-on-how-to-win-in-the-ai-era]] — Chamath Palihapitiya (8090): English as a control plane for software, the enterprise trough of disillusionment, and full rebuild rather than shallow adoption.
- [[2026-10-15-lin-stanford-ai-club-building-in-the-ai-era]] — Alfred Lin (Sequoia): the diffusion gap, value in the application layer, owning intelligence, and circular money versus leverage.

## Reading its sources

The club picks the speakers and the moderators ask friendly questions; the talks are the guests' own views, often about their own companies or portfolios. Treat them as practitioner and investor testimony, not analysis. The audience is students, so much of each talk is career advice.
