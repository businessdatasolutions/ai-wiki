# Sequoia Capital

US venture-capital firm. In this wiki Sequoia appears as a **publisher of frontier-AI interviews** — its *Training Data* podcast and *AI Ascent* event series — rather than as a technology subject. Its conversations are recurring primary sources for how AI leaders frame the field.

- [[2026-04-29-andrej-karpathy-from-vibe-coding-to-agentic-engineering|"From Vibe Coding to Agentic Engineering"]] — Sequoia AI Ascent interview with [[Andrej Karpathy]].
- [[2026-08-11-huang-sequoia-own-your-intelligence-sovereign-ai|"How Companies Are Building Their Own Intelligence"]] (August 2026) — **Sonya Huang**'s opening keynote at Sequoia's *Own Your Intelligence* event, to roughly 80 portfolio founders and AI leaders.

## The August 2026 shift: from publisher to advocate

The first two sources are Sequoia hosting other people's arguments. The third is different in kind, and worth flagging on this page: it is **Sequoia making a directional bet in its own voice and convening its portfolio to act on it**. Huang's thesis — *"not your weights, not your product"* — is that the defensible layer is migrating from the application surface to the intelligence underneath it (*"the race for the application layer"* becoming *"the race for the intelligence layer"*), and that with Kimi K3 and GLM-5.2 near the frontier, owning a model stack has flipped from a performance sacrifice to a potential performance edge. The event was structured as *"half a rallying call and half technical how-to"*, with workshops from Fireworks, LangChain, Mercor, Trajectory and Harvey.

Two things follow for how the wiki should read Sequoia sources. First, the firm is no longer only an *attribution node* here — on the sovereign-AI question it is a **party with a position**, and the companies named as evidence for that position are largely its own portfolio. Second, its house framing (*neolabs*, own-vs-rent, the application-companies-are-the-newest-labs claim) is now something the corpus should treat as **an interested framing to be tested**, not as neutral market observation. See [[2026-08-11-huang-sequoia-own-your-intelligence-sovereign-ai|the source page]] for the full scope warning.

**Sonya Huang** appears in two of the three sources — as interviewer in the Kilpatrick episode and as speaker in the keynote — but is not yet promoted to an entity page; she is not an `author:` on either (both are attributed to the channel per the wiki's video convention). Worth promoting if a third Sequoia source turns on her specifically.

Otherwise Sequoia remains an *attribution/publication* node: for the interview sources, the substantive claims live on the interview subjects' pages and the concept pages they touch.
