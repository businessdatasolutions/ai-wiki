# Morgan Stanley

US investment bank and wealth manager. In this wiki it appears in two roles: as an **adopter** of AI, in Andrew Ng's account of how it finds use cases, and as a **publisher of house views** on AI's economics, through its podcast *Thoughts on the Market*. Promoted to an entity on 2026-11-23, on its second appearance.

## Appears in this wiki via

- [[2026-07-29-ng-washington-post-china-open-source-ai-competitiveness|Ng (Washington Post Live, Jul 2026)]]: Andrew Ng cites Morgan Stanley as an adopter. The CEO, Ted Pick, set the tone, training was offered to everyone globally, and *"challenge labs"* then *"assemble business leaders and technology leaders to collaborate to find use cases."* See [[enterprise-ai-adoption]].
- [[2026-11-23-wang-xing-morgan-stanley-china-physical-ai|Wang & Xing (Thoughts on the Market, Nov 2026)]]: author and publisher. Laura Wang, chief China equity strategist at Morgan Stanley Research, and Robin Xing, chief China economist, present the firm's *Emerging Frontiers* research on physical AI in China at its 25th annual Asia-Pacific Summit in Singapore: about $12 trillion of incremental "Industry 5.0" investment over ten years, a productivity payoff from 2028, and equity earnings growth above 10% by 2030. A sell-side house view, stated without method.
