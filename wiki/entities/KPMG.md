# KPMG

Global professional-services firm (one of the Big Four). It appears in this wiki through two different survey instruments that should not be confused.

## Appears in this wiki via

- [[2025-04-28-gillespie-kpmg-trust-attitudes-and-use-of-ai-2025]] — with the University of Melbourne: a global study of trust in, attitudes to and use of AI among individuals (2025).
- [[2026-09-24-kpmg-ai-pulse-q3-2026]] — KPMG US *AI Quarterly Pulse Survey*, Q3 2026: 314 US leaders at firms with $1bn+ revenue; 58% report "measurable business value". The wording of the agent questions widens across quarters, so the quarterly series is not a clean trend.
