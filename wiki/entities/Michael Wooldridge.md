# Michael Wooldridge

**Ashall Professor of the Foundations of Artificial Intelligence at the University of Oxford.** A founding figure of multiagent-systems research, and author of the standard textbook *An Introduction to MultiAgent Systems*. His 1995 paper with Nick Jennings, "Intelligent agents: Theory and practice", is the reference the 2026 [[AI100]] report gives for the claim that *"the idea of agentic AI is far from new; the basic ideas were described about 30 years ago."* He chaired the AI100 2026 Study Panel. His research is supported by a UKRI World-Leading AI Researcher Fellowship and a Schmidt Sciences AI2050 Senior Fellowship.

In the wiki he appears in two roles about seventeen years apart:
- **The theorist of agents** whose definitions (autonomy, reactivity, pro-activeness, social ability) predate the current wave of [[ai-agents]].
- **The chair of a consensus report** on the scaling era. When speaking for himself he is more sceptical than the report: he doubts mass white-collar displacement, and he names AI-enabled attacks on poorly secured infrastructure as his top risk.
