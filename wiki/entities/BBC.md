# BBC

The **British Broadcasting Corporation**'s YouTube presence (channel: **BBC Global**), promoted to a wiki entity on its second source appearance. Both wiki sources so far are episodes of ***New Normal with Katty Kay***, a weekly explainer-interview series hosted by journalist **Katty Kay** covering how AI and other structural shifts are changing work and the economy. Distinct register from the wiki's other video sources (vendor talks, conference keynotes, academic interviews): BBC's episodes are short (8–10 minutes), journalist-hosted, and built around a single expert guest unpacking one claim or paper for a general audience.

## Coverage so far

Both episodes address **AI and employment**, from complementary but distinct angles:

- [[2026-06-22-bbc-what-if-were-wrong-about-ai-layoffs|"What If We're Wrong About AI Layoffs?"]] (Jun 2026) — with economist Kathryn Anne Edwards, on **[[ai-washing]]**: the claim that present-day layoffs are frequently over-attributed to AI for stock-valuation-narrative reasons, and that attribution is likely to stay unmeasurable.
- [[2026-07-20-bbc-researchers-studied-ai-layoffs-heres-their-warning|"Researchers studied AI layoffs. Here's their warning."]] (Jul 2026) — with Professor Gerry Tsoukalas, on **"The AI Layoff Trap"**: a game-theoretic model predicting that competitive markets could force mass AI-driven layoffs as a dominant strategy, regardless of whether any individual firm's leadership wants to avoid it.

The pairing is a shared-topic, differently-scoped set (present-attribution vs. future-prediction), not a contradiction — see [[ai-employment-effects]]'s Debates section for the fuller discussion.
