# BBC

The **British Broadcasting Corporation**'s YouTube presence, promoted to a wiki entity on its second source appearance. Two channels are now represented: **BBC Global**, carrying ***New Normal with Katty Kay*** — a weekly explainer-interview series hosted by journalist **Katty Kay** covering how AI and other structural shifts are changing work and the economy — and **BBC News**, carrying ***AI Decoded***, a longer panel programme hosted by **Christian Fraser** with recurring co-host **Dr Stephanie Hare**. Distinct register from the wiki's other video sources (vendor talks, conference keynotes, academic interviews): the *New Normal* episodes are short (8–10 minutes), journalist-hosted, and built around a single expert guest unpacking one claim or paper for a general audience; *AI Decoded* runs ~26 minutes with a three-person panel and a viewer-question segment.

## Coverage so far

The two *New Normal* episodes address **AI and employment**, from complementary but distinct angles:

- [[2026-06-22-bbc-what-if-were-wrong-about-ai-layoffs|"What If We're Wrong About AI Layoffs?"]] (Jun 2026) — with economist Kathryn Anne Edwards, on **[[ai-washing]]**: the claim that present-day layoffs are frequently over-attributed to AI for stock-valuation-narrative reasons, and that attribution is likely to stay unmeasurable.
- [[2026-07-20-bbc-researchers-studied-ai-layoffs-heres-their-warning|"Researchers studied AI layoffs. Here's their warning."]] (Jul 2026) — with Professor Gerry Tsoukalas, on **"The AI Layoff Trap"**: a game-theoretic model predicting that competitive markets could force mass AI-driven layoffs as a dominant strategy, regardless of whether any individual firm's leadership wants to avoid it.

The pairing is a shared-topic, differently-scoped set (present-attribution vs. future-prediction), not a contradiction — see [[ai-employment-effects]]'s Debates section for the fuller discussion.

The *AI Decoded* episode moves the BBC's coverage from the labour question to the **enterprise-return** question:

- [[2026-08-01-bbc-ai-decoded-why-isnt-ai-working-for-your-company|"Why isn't AI working for your company?"]] (Aug 2026) — Christian Fraser with Peter Grant (GoWeaver AI), James Kuht (Pair AI) and Dr Stephanie Hare, on the gap between $2.5tn of global AI spend and the returns on the balance sheet: a PwC survey of ~4,500 CEOs finding **56% with no ROI yet** and only one in eight able to claim a cost saving or revenue gain, a **3:1 ratio of tool access to training access** in British businesses, and the workflow-bottleneck argument that speeding one link of a chain simply piles work at the next.

Note the format difference for source-quality purposes: two of the three *AI Decoded* guests sell the remedy under discussion, which the *New Normal* single-academic-guest format avoids.
