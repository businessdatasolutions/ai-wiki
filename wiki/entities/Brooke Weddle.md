# Brooke Weddle

**Senior partner at [[McKinsey & Company]]** and a co-host of *McKinsey Talks Talent*, with [[Bryan Hancock]] and host [[Lucia Rahilly]]. In this wiki she is an interviewer whose questions and client examples shape three episodes. Promoted to an entity page on 5 October 2026, at her second appearance; she had been listed as Dangling on the first.

## What she contributes on the pages

- **[[2026-08-01-brynjolfsson-mckinsey-talks-talent-biggest-ai-opportunity|Brynjolfsson episode (Aug 2026)]]:** two organisational patterns from client work. The first is **"engine one, engine two"**: the current business, plus a separately funded AI-native disruption of it, and the discomfort of being in engine one. The second is the **mavericks** question: *"do you have a good sense of who your mavericks are, and are you strategically allocating them?"*
- **[[2026-09-29-lavoie-mckinsey-talks-talent-leading-when-change-feels-threatening|Lavoie episode (Sep 2026)]]:** she puts the episode's AI question: restructuring *"is actually more dynamic than it's ever been because of AI,"* so how does a leader's listening *"really scale"*, given people's fear for their jobs? She also recounts a CEO who shared his change narrative with his top leaders and asked for their feedback.
- **[[2026-11-24-price-mckinsey-talks-talent-employees-more-ready-for-ai|Price episode (Nov 2026)]]:** her clients keep the *enablement* horizon going while pushing top-down reinvention, *"because it helps to build trust. It also helps to upskill."* She also argues for a light *"nerve center"* of *"sophisticated multi-disciplinary colleagues"* instead of *"some heavy COE or PMO"*. And she says that with AI, unlike digital, a C-suite leader can no longer leave it to the CTO or CIO and *"stay in your lane"*. Attributions follow the camera cuts; the ASR has no speaker labels.
