# Untangle

**AI legal-workflow software for divorce attorneys and family-law firms**, founded and run solo by **[[Ryan Carson]]**. Positioned as *"AI for divorce attorneys,"* addressing three named pain points: **discovery chaos, paralegal shortages, and administrative busy work**. Serves matters from standard cases to high-asset and complex divorces.

Untangle's significance to this wiki is as the **limit case of the AI-native company**: a venture-scale B2B SaaS product shipping **22–40 pull requests a day** with **one human employee** and a fleet of cloud agents in place of an engineering, QA and customer-success team. Where most AI-native-org material describes managers running agent-augmented *teams*, Untangle has removed the team and kept only the management layer.

Its AI paralegal product is named **Grace** — the surface graded nightly by Carson's self-improvement automation loop, where substandard conversations trigger child agents that open fix PRs.

**Named on the company's About page:** Linda Douglas, Chief Legal Officer (38-year family-law veteran, nearly 2,000 cases) and Renée C. Bauer, Divorce Attorney Partner (founder of Happy Even After Family Law). Carson's stated goal: *"make divorce feel less confusing for families, and less chaotic for the attorneys helping them."*

At the time of the August 2026 material Carson described himself as at *"max Q"* — maximum pressure to hire — and was running an open engineering role with no phone screen and no interview, judged on a screen-recording of the candidate managing agents.
