# Tanguy Catlin

Senior partner at **[[McKinsey & Company]]** (Boston office) and a director of the **[[McKinsey Global Institute]]**. He appears in three sources, all from 2026: twice as an interviewee and once as an author.

Promoted to an entity page on 27 November 2026. His two earlier pages listed him as Dangling with the note *"two sources each, never `author:`."* The McKinsey Quarterly article made him an author, on his third source.

## What he contributes

- **[[2026-07-08-de-smet-mckinsey-three-horizons-of-ai-transformation|From adoption to impact (McKinsey Quarterly, 8 Jul 2026)]]:** coauthor of the readiness survey. It reports three horizons of AI maturity and a 70% / 27% gap between personal and organizational readiness. Organizational readiness accounts for about twice as much of reported value as personal readiness.
- **[[2026-07-09-catlin-mckinsey-podcast-real-ai-advantage|The real AI advantage (The McKinsey Podcast, 9 Jul 2026)]]:** productivity gains from a general-purpose technology get competed away. The advantage lies in redesigning a domain end to end (McKinsey's *Rewired*), not in adding AI to existing processes.
- **[[2026-10-27-catlin-durth-hamalainen-montard-mckinsey-ceo-agenda-agentic-age|The CEO's agenda for the agentic age (Inside the Strategy Room, 27 Oct 2026)]]:** *"that's where you lose the battle"* about an obsessive focus on productivity. Deploying tools gives about 20%, redesign multiples of that. Most organizations have not codified their workflows.

## Recurring position

Redesign over deployment, in all three sources. The two interviews argue it from competition: everyone has the same models. The article argues it from survey data: workflow redesign and organizational redesign are the factors most linked to reported value. Being one author's view, the three do not count as independent confirmation of each other.
