# Deloitte

Global professional-services firm (one of the Big Four). In this wiki it appears as the publisher of executive surveys on AI. Both are self-reported by executives and run by a firm that sells AI advice, so read them as directional.

## Appears in this wiki via

- [[2026-01-21-deloitte-state-of-ai-in-the-enterprise-2026]] — Deloitte AI Institute, *State of AI in the Enterprise* (January 2026; 3,235 leaders in 24 countries, fieldwork August–September 2025). 25% have moved 40% or more of their AI experiments into production; 21% have a mature governance model for autonomous agents.
- [[2025-12-11-deloitte-2026-global-health-care-outlook]] — *2026 Global Health Care Outlook* (December 2025): 180 executives of large health systems in six countries, the Netherlands included. About 30% use generative AI at scale in some areas; 2% across the whole organisation; 3% report significant ROI.
