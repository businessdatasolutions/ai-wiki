# IBM

Technology and consulting company. Its Institute for Business Value (IBV) publishes executive surveys; IBM sells AI products and services, so its survey findings are vendor research.

## Appears in this wiki via

- [[2025-05-06-ibm-ceo-study-2025]] — IBV *CEO Study 2025* (2,000 CEOs, 33 geographies, February–April 2025): CEOs estimate that 25% of their AI initiatives delivered the expected ROI and 16% were scaled enterprise-wide. The unit is initiatives, not organisations.
- [[2026-09-15-krishna-bain-winning-with-ai-era-of-experimentation-is-over]] — Arvind Krishna, IBM's chairman and CEO, on Bain's *Winning with AI* podcast.
