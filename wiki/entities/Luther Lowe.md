# Luther Lowe

**Head of public policy at [[Y Combinator]].** In this wiki he is the host, not a subject: the MC of YC Root Access's [[2026-09-30-yc-root-access-startup-industrial-base-dc|The Startup Industrial Base]], a full day of founders and US officials on defense in Washington, D.C. (30 September 2026). Promoted to an entity page on 3 October 2026, after appearing on two source pages from that event.

## What he does on the pages

- **Introduces the speakers.** His introduction of Under Secretary Emil Michael, *"the under secretary of war for research and engineering at the department and the department's chief technology officer"*, is where the [[2026-09-30-michael-miller-department-of-war-ai-adoption|Michael & Miller]] page takes its speaker roles from.
- **Closes the day.** In his closing remarks (7:57:52) he returns to [[Garry Tan]]'s opening question and recaps three answers from the day: competitions with clear standards and actual orders (Michael), bring a solution and not just a technology (Duffy), and exciting a user and finding a buyer *"are two different jobs"* (Hurst).
