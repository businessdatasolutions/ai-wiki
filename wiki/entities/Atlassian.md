# Atlassian

Software company behind **Jira** (work tracking, used widely outside software teams) and **Confluence** (documents and whiteboards). Its co-founder Mike (Cannon-Brookes, per the episode description) is CEO.

## In the wiki

- [[2026-11-22-mansour-lennys-podcast-everyone-becoming-a-builder|Sherif Mansour on Lenny's Podcast (Nov 2026)]]: its Head of AI and Product Craft on the company's PM craft, its AI-builder squads (reverted within a month), hiring more juniors than seniors, agents added to Jira workflows (*"nearly 10 million agents… in loops every month"* in Jira and Confluence, self-reported), an open-by-default culture with recorded exec reviews, and a "team anywhere" policy.
- [[2026-05-22-everitt-jetbrains-deeplearningai-ai-dev-26-sf-shift-to-agentic-engineering|Everitt (JetBrains) at AI Dev 26]]: names Atlassian among firms with large layoffs in May 2026.
- [[2026-06-25-raboresearch-ai-it-zakelijke-dienstverlening|RaboResearch (June 2026)]]: cites Atlassian Research 2025, a firm-level study in which 96% report no ROI from AI.

The episode and the two mentions are not reconciled in any source: Mansour does not discuss headcount.
