# HubSpot

A US software company selling a CRM platform with marketing, sales and customer-service tools, aimed mainly at small and mid-sized businesses. Its annual conference, **UNBOUND** (formerly INBOUND), is published on the **HubSpot Live** YouTube channel; source pages written from that channel carry `author: ["HubSpot Live"]` under the video convention that the channel is the author. This page covers both the company and the channel.

HubSpot matters to this wiki for one reason more than any other: **it is a firm whose own funnel was among the first hit by the move from search to AI answers, and it reports the numbers.** It is also a vendor selling the tools for the response, so its figures come with a stake.
