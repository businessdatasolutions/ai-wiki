# Brett Hemenway Falk

**University of Pennsylvania.** Co-author, with **[[Gerry Tsoukalas]]** (Boston University), of **"The AI Layoff Trap"** (arXiv:2603.20617) — a task-based game-theoretic model showing that competitive markets can trap firms in an automation arms race that destroys the consumer demand every firm depends on, even when every individual firm foresees the collective harm. First named in the wiki (as "Brett Falk") on [[2026-07-20-bbc-researchers-studied-ai-layoffs-heres-their-warning|BBC's "New Normal with Katty Kay" episode]] popularizing the paper, though he was not himself interviewed; promoted to an entity page once [[2026-06-03-falk-tsoukalas-the-ai-layoff-trap|the underlying paper]] gave him a second source.
