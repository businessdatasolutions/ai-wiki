# Karim Lakhani

Dorothy & Michael Hintze Professor of Business Administration at Harvard Business School; chair of the Digital Data Design Institute at Harvard; co-founder of the Laboratory for Innovation Science at Harvard.

Co-author on the [[2026-04-28-dellacqua-jagged-technological-frontier|Jagged Technological Frontier]] paper (Dell'Acqua et al., *Org Sci* 2026), the [[2026-06-12-dellacqua-cybernetic-teammate-field-experiment-genai-teamwork|Cybernetic Teammate]] field experiment, and [[2026-04-29-boussioux-crowdless-future|The Crowdless Future]].

As of 2026 his HBS bio calls him co-founder and faculty chair of the [[HBS AI Institute]], apparently the renamed Digital Data Design Institute. Lead author of *A Guide to Building Change Resilience in the Age of AI* (HBR, July 2025, with Jen Stave, Doug Ng and Daniel Martines), known in the wiki through the institute's [[2025-08-05-hbs-ai-institute-mastering-change-resilience-key-to-ai-driven-success|summary]]: three organisational "muscles" (sensing, rewiring, lock-in) and a five-step playbook (Learn, Do, Imagine, Act, Care).

## Second appearance: the P&G field experiment (added 2026-09-15)

Co-author on [[2026-06-12-dellacqua-cybernetic-teammate-field-experiment-genai-teamwork|*The Cybernetic Teammate*]] (*Organization Science* 37(4), 2026) — the preregistered 791-person experiment at Procter & Gamble with [[Fabrizio Dell'Acqua]] and [[Ethan Mollick]], testing whether generative AI can substitute for a human teammate across performance, expertise integration and social engagement.

## On Silicon Valley Girl (added 2026-11-04)

[[2026-10-30-lakhani-silicon-valley-girl-people-most-at-risk-from-ai|Interview on Silicon Valley Girl (Oct 2026)]]: his first appearance in the wiki as a speaker rather than an author. He restates the cyborg, centaur and self-automator modes (*"self-automators do the worst because they have stopped thinking"*), describes HBS's required AI course (900 MBA students vibe coding their final projects) and executive programmes in which the C-suite builds an app in 90 minutes, and reports unpublished usage data showing a *"K curve"* inside large firms. His view that the middle layer, not juniors, is most at risk runs against [[2026-04-28-brynjolfsson-canaries-coal-mine|Canaries in the Coal Mine]]. *"I have fired people who have told me the AI gave me that."*
