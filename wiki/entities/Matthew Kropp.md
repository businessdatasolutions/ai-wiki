# Matthew Kropp

**Managing Director & Senior Partner at [[Boston Consulting Group]]** and a BCG Henderson Institute author, introduced in October 2026 as **CTO of BCG X** (the May 2026 HBR byline gave chief AI officer of BCG X); lead voice in BCG's research on AI's organizational and labor-market impact. Promoted to an entity on 2026-06-15 on his second wiki appearance.

## Wiki contributions

- [[2026-05-06-kropp-bcg-hbr-dont-treat-ai-agents-like-employees|Kropp et al. 2026 (HBR)]] — lead author of the N=1,261 randomized experiment showing the *AI-as-employee framing* causes a 9pp accountability drop and more escalations; the wiki's sharpest anti-anthropomorphizing-AI source.
- [[2026-04-03-bcg-emerson-kropp-ai-will-reshape-more-jobs-than-it-replaces|Emerson, Kropp et al. 2026]] — co-author of *AI Will Reshape More Jobs Than It Replaces*: the six **AI Labor Disruption Segments**, the 50–55%-reshaped / 10–15%-eliminated split, and the substitution-vs-augmentation × demand-expandability model.
- [[2026-10-06-kropp-bcg-so-what-lessons-from-an-all-ai-company|Kropp on The So What from BCG (Oct 2026)]] — guest on *token-based competition*: BCG/Cursor token data on 107 public companies (top quintile by consumption grew 15%, bottom 5%; correlation), *return on intelligence* as a project ratio, an all-agent company he ran and shut down, and his estimate that under 10% of engineers in large firms use agentic coding tools effectively, with identity threat as the main barrier.
