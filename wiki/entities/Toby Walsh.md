# Toby Walsh

**ARC Laureate Fellow and Scientia Professor of AI at the University of New South Wales.** He sits on the Steering Committee of the [[AI Index]] and was a member of the [[AI100]] 2026 Study Panel, one of two people (with [[Raymond Perrault]]) who author both Stanford HAI series. That overlap is why the wiki treats agreement between the two reports as not independent. AI100 acknowledges his ARC Laureate Fellowship (FL200100204).
