# Glenn R. Carroll

The **Adams Distinguished Professor of Management** at [[Stanford GSB|Stanford Graduate School of Business]]. Organization theorist and sociologist; long-running collaborator with [[Jesper B. Sørensen]] on the theory-based view of strategy and analogical reasoning.

Two sources in the wiki:

- *[[2026-04-28-carroll-sorensen-strategy-analogy|Strategy Theory Using Analogy: Rationale, Tools and Examples]]* (*Strategy Science* 9, 2024) — co-authored with [[Jesper B. Sørensen]]; anchors [[analogical-reasoning]] and [[theory-based-view]].
- *[[2026-06-25-carroll-stanford-gsb-making-organizational-culture-great|"On Making Organizational Culture Great"]]* (Stanford GSB GSBooks session, 25 Jun 2026) — presenting his book with **Jennifer Chatman** (Dean, Haas School of Business). Strong culture = **high intensity + high agreement**; culture as a **social-control system aligned to strategy**; the wiki's authoritative anchor for the `strategic-renewal/organizational-culture` cell on [[warner-wager-process-model]].
