# Ishit Vachhrajani

**Director of Technology and AI, and Executive in Residence at [[Amazon Web Services|AWS]]**, based in New York (~25 years working in the city). Originates the **four mental-model shifts** for the agentic era — operational-execution culture → research-lab culture; govern-through-gates → guardrails; factory-floor management → trading-floor management; functional silos → immune-system responsiveness. The framework was first credited to him secondhand, on the [[2025-12-02-allen-brozovich-davis-aws-reinvent-advanced-team-structures|AWS re:Invent 2025]] source page, where [[Steven Brovich|Stephen Brozovich]] restates it crediting "a same-conference session by Ishit Vachhrajani." He delivers the framework first-hand and in full — reorganized as a three-pillar organization/trust/culture keynote — at [[2026-07-09-nyc-executive-forum-2026-a-leaders-guide-to-agentic-ai|AWS NYC Executive Forum 2026]], where the auto-generated transcript renders his surname "Ishit Washani."

The NYC talk is a **close cousin, not a re-delivery**, of the AWS Enterprise Strategy "advanced team structures" franchise ([[2025-12-02-allen-brozovich-davis-aws-reinvent-advanced-team-structures|re:Invent]] → [[2026-05-21-allen-aws-london-exec-forum-agentic-team-structures|London]] → [[2026-06-12-aws-leaders-guide-advanced-team-structures-agentic-world|Sydney]]): same AWS Enterprise Strategy doctrine (hourglass organization, Singapore governance, Bedrock AgentCore, expert-generalist/renaissance-developer talent framing, non-determinism-as-feature), independently reached and reorganized around his own framework rather than Jonathan Allen's USE/COMPOSE/BUILD economics.
