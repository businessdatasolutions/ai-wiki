# David Deming

Labour economist at Harvard: Danoff Dean of Harvard College, William Henry Bloomberg Professor of Economics, and Isabel and Scott Black Professor of Public Policy at Harvard Kennedy School (titles as read out at the [[2026-11-19-raimondo-holcomb-harvard-iop-managing-unemployment-ai-economy|Institute of Politics forum]]). His work in the wiki concerns social and leadership skills and the measurement of AI's spread.

## Appears in this wiki via

- [[2025-08-05-weidmann-xu-deming-measuring-leadership-skills-ai-agents]] — co-author, with Ben Weidmann and Yixian Xu (Harvard Skills Lab), of a pre-registered experiment showing that leading a team of AI agents measures the same skill as leading a team of people. Summarised by the [[HBS AI Institute]] in [[2026-09-03-hbs-ai-institute-think-youre-a-good-leader-try-leading-an-ai-team]].
- [[2026-11-19-raimondo-holcomb-harvard-iop-managing-unemployment-ai-economy]] — moderates Gina Raimondo and Eric Holcomb of Raise US. He sets the uncertainty: unemployment at 4.1%, 5.2% for new college graduates, *"historically low"*, against predictions of white-collar job loss, and asks how a crisis of unknown timing should change policy.
- The Real-Time Population Survey with Alexander Bick and Adam Blandin, the generative-AI adoption series cited in [[2026-07-01-stanford-del-adoption-monitor]] and [[2026-04-03-allen-fed-feds-note-monitoring-ai-adoption-us-economy]] (both now link this page).
