Verification is the level-gate

Juniors adopt AI fastest and judge its output worst; experts extract more because they can evaluate. A model keyed on usage alone ranks the confident non-verifier above the careful evaluator — backwards.

Mollick 2026 · Kiron & Schrage 2026 · Brynjolfsson 2026

Fluency is learnable — and visible

Experienced users delegate less blindly, iterate and validate more, match model to task, and succeed +4 pp more on the same tasks. The behaviours below are the ones that move.

Anthropic Economic Index 5, 2026

Self-report is a biased instrument

Developers measured 19% slower with AI believed they were 20% faster. Answer with what you actually did recently — not with who you'd like to be.
