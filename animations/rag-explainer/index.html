How RAG finds its context

A question is embedded into the same vector space as the document fragments. The nearest fragments attach to it, and the question carries them out of the space into the prompt the LLM answers from. Three questions, three retrievals.

What happens each round

  1. Fragments are embedded. Every snippet in the knowledge base becomes a point. Snippets about the same topic land close together.
  2. A question is asked and embedded with the same model, so it becomes a point in that same space.
  3. Distances are measured. Closer means more similar in meaning. The numbers on the lines are similarity scores.
  4. The top-k nearest are kept (k = 3 here); everything else is ignored.
  5. The question leaves the space and drags its snippets along into the prompt.
  6. The LLM answers using the question plus the retrieved context.

The three questions

COOKING

How long should I boil an egg?

Two strong egg matches, plus a weak third about pasta water. Top-k always returns k results, relevant or not.

SPACE

Why is Mars red?

Lands in the astronomy cluster; the iron-oxide fragment is the nearest.

HEALTH + COOKING

Is eating eggs every day healthy?

Sits between two topics, so retrieval mixes fragments from both clusters.
